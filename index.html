Protect, Preserve, Empower

Black history is a vital part of the story of humanity, yet many of the records, artifacts, and stories that document the experiences of people of African descent are at risk of being lost, ignored, or erased. The Black History Foundation is committed to protecting this legacy by preserving historical records, supporting education, and empowering communities to safeguard their own stories.

Through research, digital preservation, community partnerships, and educational initiatives, we work to ensure that the achievements, struggles, and contributions of Black communities remain visible and accessible for generations to come.

This platform also allows the public to monitor federal policies and legislation that affect Black history preservation, providing access to policy analysis, impact summaries, and tools that help citizens understand how government actions may influence the protection of cultural heritage.

History shapes identity. When we protect our past, we strengthen our future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower the African diaspora through education, cultural celebration, and the preservation of our history. We believe that protecting our past is the key to shaping a stronger, more unified future.

Vision: A future where Black history is accurately preserved and integrated into mainstream narratives, where cultural heritage is celebrated, and where every person of African descent has access to their historical roots.

Cultural Heritage

Preserving cultural heritage means safeguarding the stories, traditions, artifacts, and historical records that reflect the experiences and contributions of people of African descent. Through archival preservation, community storytelling, cultural events, and partnerships with historians and institutions, we work to ensure that Black history is not lost, distorted, or forgotten. By protecting these cultural treasures, we strengthen identity, deepen historical understanding, and ensure that future generations can learn directly from the legacy of those who came before them.

Innovation

Innovation allows us to use modern tools and creative approaches to protect and share Black history in powerful new ways. By leveraging digital archives, interactive media, data preservation technologies, and collaborative platforms, we expand access to historical knowledge beyond traditional classrooms and museums. Our goal is to ensure that Black history remains visible, accessible, and engaging for future generations by combining historical scholarship with forward-thinking solutions that reach communities around the world.

Community

Community is at the center of preserving and celebrating Black history. We believe that history lives through people—through families, neighborhoods, educators, elders, and young leaders who carry stories forward. By fostering collaboration, dialogue, and community-driven initiatives, we bring people together to share experiences, preserve local histories, and build a collective commitment to protecting our cultural legacy. Strong communities ensure that the lessons of the past continue to inspire progress, unity, and empowerment.

Education

Education is the foundation for understanding the significance of Black history and its impact on society today. We support educational programs, curriculum development, workshops, and public learning initiatives that make accurate historical knowledge accessible to students, educators, and communities. By providing resources that highlight the achievements, struggles, and contributions of people of African descent, we empower individuals to learn from history, think critically about the present, and help shape a more informed and equitable future.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 'Save Black History' campaign?
Our campaign to safeguard Black history from being lost, erased, or misrepresented. We digitize documents, restore landmarks, and fund educational programs.
How can I get involved as a volunteer?
Join as a Research Assistant, Community Outreach volunteer, Digital Content Creator, or Fundraising volunteer. Visit our volunteer page for opportunities.
Where does my donation go?
Donations fund digitization, landmark restoration, educational programs, and community workshops.
Do you accept all types of payments?
Yes. We accept credit card, debit card, and bank transfer via our donation form, and Bitcoin (Lightning and on-chain) on this site.
Do you accept cryptocurrency donations?
Yes. We accept Bitcoin (Lightning and on-chain) and traditional fiat payments via our donation form.
Is my donation tax-deductible?
Please consult your tax advisor. The Black History Foundation is a 501(c)(3) organization where applicable.
